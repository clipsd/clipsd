- Update: from the next update on, the program reopens after the restart all screenshots that were open, including fresh ones without marks, with the same screenshot shape. A window you closed yourself before the restart does not come back.
- Hide personal data: a photo inserted with “From phone” on top of a screenshot is checked too, at its full resolution. The card number and name on it are found even when an IBAN has already been found on the screenshot itself.
- The “Open separately” button of an inserted picture is now blue and larger, easy to see on top of a photo.
- Screenshot shape: rounded corners, oval and circle can now be made smaller or bigger. Drag the handles on the corners and sides of the shape frame (Shift keeps proportions, a circle always stays a circle), and drag the area around the shape to move it. The finished picture is cropped to the shape frame, and the shadow, frame and margin go around it.
- The screenshot shape can now be chosen right in the selection window: the Screenshot shape tile on the panel (a click turns on the last shape, the arrow opens all shapes) or the F key. The frame shows the shape outline, Copy and close and Save give a picture with the shape, and the editor opens with it already applied. Each new selection starts as a rectangle.
- “From phone”: if the computer reconnects to another network, the window shows a new code with the new address by itself. If the network is gone, the window waits and opens reception as soon as the network is back.
